\section{StructPF Prompt Structure}
\label{app:structpf-prompts}

Tables~\ref{tab:app-structpf-2a-propose} to~\ref{tab:app-structpf-3-write} summarize the StructPF prompts for Stages~2a, 2b, and~3, one table per prompt. Each prompt has a system message with the rules and output format, and a user message with the case-specific inputs. Stage~1 is a database query and has no prompt. The full text is in \texttt{src/generation/prompts/} (\texttt{design\_briefs.yaml} and \texttt{design\_data.yaml}).

Purpose & Propose one internal design brief that gives students a challenge without stating the target concept. \\

\footnotesize

\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Summary} \\
\midrule
Purpose & Choose the most promising candidate brief for these learners, or name the concern that most needs revision. \\
Key rules & A successful familiar route is not a rejection reason. \newline Flag a candidate whose student-facing fields announce the discovery, prescribe the target claim, or presuppose it. \newline Do not invent missing assumptions to rescue a candidate. \newline If every candidate discloses the idea, choose the best and name the specific revision needed. \\

\begin{table}[!htb]
\centering
\caption[Stage 2a quantity translation prompt structure (\texttt{TRANSLATE\_BRIEF})]{Stage~2a quantity translation prompt structure (\texttt{TRANSLATE\_BRIEF}).}
\label{tab:app-structpf-2a-translate}
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.5pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.16}
\begin{tabular}{@{} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.20\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.72\textwidth} @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Summary} \\
\midrule
Purpose & Encode the selected brief as technical quantities and checkable conditions, without redesigning it. \\
Key rules & Preserve the whole goal, assumptions, and allowed actions. \newline Do not shrink an allocation to one recipient or replace an invention goal with a prescribed formula. \newline Declare the given inputs and derived quantities that the relationships need. \newline Instantiate one concrete case with fixed positive list lengths when counts are open. \\
Inputs & \texttt{design\_brief}, \texttt{selection}, \texttt{target\_concept}, \texttt{grade}, \texttt{prior\_context}, \texttt{translation\_feedback}; \texttt{quantity\_contract} and \texttt{checker\_contract} (system) \\
Output & JSON quantity objects and an optional \texttt{prior\_contrast} intent, with \texttt{status} and \texttt{reason} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\begin{table}[!htb]
\centering
\caption[Stage 2b number proposal prompt structure (\texttt{PROPOSE\_DATA})]{Stage~2b number proposal prompt structure (\texttt{PROPOSE\_DATA}).}
\label{tab:app-structpf-2b-propose}
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.5pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.16}
\begin{tabular}{@{} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.20\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.72\textwidth} @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Summary} \\
\midrule
Purpose & Choose concrete numbers, and calculator conditions that check them, for the selected design. \\
Key rules & Choose numbers only; preserve the goal, relationships, allowed actions, and question. \newline Link each essential requirement to a condition with an executable calculation and predicate. \newline Use lowercase quantity names and finite numeric values. \\
Inputs & \texttt{design\_brief}, \texttt{requirements}, \texttt{student\_context}, \texttt{prior\_context}, \texttt{feedback} \\
Output & JSON with \texttt{quantities}, \texttt{values}, and \texttt{conditions} (statement, requirement indices, calculation, reasoning) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Summary} \\
\midrule
Purpose & Adjust only the numerical values after the calculator reports exact results. \\
Key rules & Keep names, units, list sizes, design, formulas, and predicates unchanged. \newline Do not remove a failed condition or change the successful route to pass a check. \newline Return actual numbers, not formulas. \\

Output & JSON \texttt{values} object containing every original name \\

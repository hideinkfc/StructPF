Productive Failure (PF) is a well-established learning design in which students attempt challenging, ill-structured problems before receiving instruction, generating diverse representations and solution methods (RSMs) that prepare them for deeper learning \cite{kapur2008,sinha2021}. However, designing effective PF exercises remains labor-intensive, requiring careful calibration of ill-structuredness across multiple dimensions \cite{kapur2009}.

We propose an LLM-based pipeline that automatically generates PF exercises and evaluates their quality through simulated student interactions.

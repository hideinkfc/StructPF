\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
First and foremost, I would like to thank my supervisor Jakub Macina, who accompanied this thesis from the first proposal to the last figure. Whenever I got stuck, he had a paper to point me to, a resource I did not know existed, or an idea that made me look at the problem from a different side. Many of the directions in this thesis began as a short remark in one of our meetings, and I am grateful for the time and patience he put into them.

Deciding what makes a good Productive Failure task turned out to be a rather subjective question, and at times it was really hard to work on something without a clear right answer. Writing a thesis about Productive Failure also meant that I failed productively quite a few times myself, and I am thankful to everyone who supported me during this journey.

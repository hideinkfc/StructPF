01

Two autoresearch questions

1. Do high model scores hide problems in the same tasks?
2. Does grounding generation in curriculum evidence help?

Results from 13 October 2026. Every judgment here is from a model or a rule, not from human raters or students.

The answers at a glance

Why we asked

Earlier reviews found faults in a different set of tasks. That did not show whether the high scores on the same tasks were misleading.

The rule

A concern counts only when both judges name the same property and quote the task exactly. A single judge's flag stays a disputed candidate.

08

Question 2 interpretation and corrections

What the data support

Curriculum had the highest support on target relevance, distinct ideas and useful contrast. Learning Commons had the highest choice support, but lower target relevance, distinct ideas and useful contrast than curriculum.

What they do not support

A general quality gain. The primary difference was one task, and 59 of 60 primary outcomes were unresolved.

What went wrong before the result was trusted

StepFinding
First auditThe evaluator dropped the taught-history input; 0 of 60 accessible starts could be assessed. Kept as a failed record
